# What a Manychat subscription buys

**November 26, 2026.** For a creator whose main job is “someone comments GUIDE and gets the link,” the first meaningful costs are contact overages, hosting, and the time needed to keep an Instagram app connected. A team that also needs campaign broadcasts, assignment queues, carrier delivery and multichannel reporting is buying considerably more.

## Pricing in USD

The web subscription introduced March 2, 2026 counts distinct people who engage in a billing month. The allowance resets each billing month, including for annual subscriptions. With annual billing the entire base price is paid up front. Region, migration status and purchase surface matter. The public pricing page served a legacy variant during this audit. The individual new-plan articles below supply the USD figures. ## Services and what a replacement needs

| Service | Manychat's documented behavior | Magic Word implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram comments | Select a post or reel, match a comment, send the first private reply and optionally answer in public. Subsequent messages depend on user interaction and the platform window. | Keyword rules for one post, all posts or the next post. Public replies, private replies, delivery queue, deduplication and a no-send matcher. |
| Instagram DMs, Stories and profile | DM keywords, default replies, story replies and mentions, conversation starters, menu, referral links, ads and eligible growth triggers. | Keyword, first inbound DM, story reply and mention flows, menu and starters. No feature claim for follower, ad or share-to-story partner events. |
| Flow builder | Triggers, messages, actions, conditions, delays, branches, data collection, testing, templates, sequencing and publishing. | Browser graph editor and editable MCP graphs with buttons, quick replies, delays, tags, conditions and weighted splits. Draft, test, publish, copy, move and restore. Manychat template files are not interoperable. |
| Contacts and lead capture | Tags, custom fields, segmentation, filtering, bulk actions, user input and export/integration. | Search, tags, custom contact fields and paginated reads for agents. An agent can store a supplied email or consent fact. A full channel-aware input form, unsubscribe system and CRM sync require additional implementation. |
| Inbox | Shared agent inbox, conversation assignment, groups, manual takeover, routing, saved context and performance reporting. | Owner inbox and a restricted reviewer login for Instagram. Pause, resume, react, reply, inspect threads, tag and read metrics. Additional channel text conversations appear through MCP. No equivalent to Manychat's team assignment or SLA reporting. |
| Messenger | Page conversations, comments and ads triggers, opt-in entry points and broadcasts where allowed. | Signed Page text-message webhooks, keyword rules, manual replies, time-window checks and delivery records. Facebook comment-to-DM and marketing broadcasts are outside this release. |
| WhatsApp | Number onboarding, migration/coexistence choices, opt-ins, approved templates, broadcasts and handoff. | Cloud API inbound text and customer-service replies for a configured phone number. Approved-template campaigns, broadcast audiences and onboarding migration remain separate. Meta template rates depend on category and recipient market. |
| Telegram | Bot connection, inbound messages and automation entry points. | Bot API text webhooks, keyword replies and operator replies in recently active private conversations. |
| TikTok | Business messaging, supported triggers, QR/referral entry points and handoff. Features vary by connection, region and API access. | No TikTok transport. The public product page alone cannot establish an independent app's access. |
| SMS and email | Twilio SMS/MMS with consent and carrier charges. Email involves sending-domain setup, audience permission and delivery management. | No SMS carrier or email delivery service. A contact field can hold a voluntarily provided address. |
| AI | Knowledge, intent recognition, goals, AI steps and replies, comments, flow assistance and copy editing. | Rules and operator MCP work without a model key. **Two easy mistakes when rebuilding.** A new follower and a first inbound DM are different events. Meta determines eligibility for Manychat's Follow to DM beta. Also, an opening private reply to a comment is different from an interactive reply that opens a conversation. An ordinary website-link click is insufficient for some follow-up workflows. Treat these as separate events in the state machine. ## What the public API establishes

REA 6.10.1 inspected both OpenAPI 3.0 documents linked by Manychat's public Swagger UI. The Page contract declares 34 paths covering page information, tags, fields, flows, subscribers and sending. The Profile contract declares one template-link path. Those declarations establish a public integration surface. They provide no evidence about the vendor's database, queue implementation, performance, or the entitlements of a particular account. [Official Swagger UI](https://api.manychat.com/swagger)

## What free hosting means here

Magic Word has no software subscription or per-contact meter. A machine you already run can host Node and SQLite. Electricity, backups, connectivity and administration still have a cost. The Cloudflare route uses Workers, D1, Queues and a Durable Object. Published free limits include 100,000 Worker requests/day, five million D1 rows read/day, 100,000 rows written/day, 5 GB total D1 storage and 10,000 queue operations/day. Enqueuing, reading and deleting may each consume an operation. Background jobs count even on a quiet account. Workers Paid begins at $5/month plus any excess usage. AI inference, provider charges and a new VPS or domain may add costs. There is no verified rate at which a specific account will exhaust these quotas.
